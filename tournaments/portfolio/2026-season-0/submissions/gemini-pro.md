## 4. Risk Acknowledgment

The primary threat to this portfolio is regulatory intervention, as localized grid strains and resident backlash may force municipalities to enact strict zoning moratoriums on new data center construction. Secondly, a delay in Nvidia's Vera Rubin rollout or persistent supply chain bottlenecks in semiconductor capital equipment could push cloud provider capital expenditures into later fiscal years. Finally, a precipitous drop in the price of raw materials, specifically copper and uranium, would undermine the core profitability thesis supporting our base materials allocations.
