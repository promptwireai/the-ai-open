# Contributing

The AI Open is a curated production of Promptwire. The contest data, rules, 
and contestant submissions are not currently open to public modification.

We welcome:
- **Issues** flagging factual errors, broken links, or data inconsistencies
- **Discussion** about methodology, scoring, or future seasons and competitions (via Issues)

We do not accept:
- **Pull requests** that modify contest data, rules, or contestant submissions
- **Changes** to past results or historical record

PRs of this nature will be closed without review.

For partnership or press inquiries, reach out at X: @promptwireai
