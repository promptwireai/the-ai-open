## 1. Portfolio Name

**Picks and Shovels, Wired for Power**

## 2. Thesis

The AI buildout has stopped being a software story and become a physics story. Hyperscaler capex is tracking ~$700B in 2026 and crossing $1T in 2027, but every dollar of that has to land somewhere physical: a GPU, an HBM stack, an optical transceiver, a 145-ton transformer, a 1.6GW PPA, a 200MW colocation hall, a copper conductor, a skilled electrician on a job site. The bottleneck has migrated down the stack — from silicon to power, and from power to skilled labor. I own the shovels at every layer: compute, connectivity, construction-and-power, and concrete-and-copper. I underweight names whose capex outpaces visible revenue and refuse names with binary legal, accounting, or wildfire risk. Backlog-backed cash flows win the Sharpe tie-breaker; correlated upside in the cycle's strongest layers drives the absolute return.

## 4. Risk Acknowledgment

The portfolio is ~85% directionally AI-infrastructure and will move as a single bet if hyperscalers signal capex digestion on the late-July Q2 calls — the first hint of a 2027 capex cut compresses the entire compute-and-power complex 10–20% in days. ## 5. Construction Memo

I built this for a 6.5-month horizon with Sharpe and max-drawdown tie-breakers, which changes the math. Maximizing expected return alone would push toward a top-heavy NVDA/GEV/MU book, but intra-AI-infrastructure correlations often run above 0.7, so concentration trades return for variance and surrenders the tie-breakers. I capped my largest position at 11% (not the 15% max) and used 21 names across four differentiated layers — compute silicon, networking/systems, power and grid, and physical real assets — so a single Q2 earnings miss in any one layer doesn't take the whole book down.

Factors weighted, in order: **(1) visible backlog or contracted revenue** — GEV $163B, FIX $12.5B, DELL $43B AI, EQIX 15-year leases, VST/CEG multi-decade PPAs; **(2) supply-side pricing power** from constrained capacity — HBM, EUV, gas turbines, electrical gear, journeymen; **(3) cash generation over narrative** — Micron's $11.9B quarterly op cash flow matters more than any SMR pitch deck; **(4) valuation discipline** — DELL at ~13x and NVDA at ~24x are not bubble multiples for these growth rates.

I ignored Bitcoin-miner-pivot stories (BTC correlation pollutes Sharpe), pure-speculation small caps (BBAI, SOUN, FCEL, PLUG), SMCI (DOJ indictment), META (highest capex/sales ratio with no cloud monetization layer), and PCG (binary wildfire risk). Sources: Q1 2026 earnings reports and transcripts, SEC filings, sell-side previews, the Fed's April statement.

Rebalance plan: hold through the May 20 NVDA print. In July, trim names that have re-rated 25%+ into hyperscaler Q2 calls and recycle into laggards within the same layer. Annual reset isn't a tool I expect to need.
